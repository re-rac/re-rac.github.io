ReRAC is a fan-made PC port of Ratchet & Clank, the 2002 PlayStation 2 game. You play it through the ReRAC Launcher,
which reads the game from your own disc once, keeps ReRAC up to date and starts it for you.

<Aside type="caution" title="Not released yet">
ReRAC is still in development and there's nothing to download yet. These pages describe how it will work.
</Aside>
