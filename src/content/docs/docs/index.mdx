**ReRAC** is an unofficial, native PC port of Ratchet & Clank (2002, PlayStation 2),
built in Rust on the Bevy engine from a decompilation of the original game. It is not an emulator.

You play it through the **ReRAC Launcher**. The launcher extracts the game data from an image of your own disc,
then installs, updates and starts versions of the game.

<Aside type="caution" title="Nothing is released yet">
The game and the launcher are early and in active development, and neither has been released. These pages describe
how things work today and how they are meant to work at release. Downloads are coming later.
</Aside>
