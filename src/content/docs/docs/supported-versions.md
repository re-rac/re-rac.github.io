---
title: Supported versions
description: Which copy of Ratchet & Clank works with ReRAC.
---

For now, ReRAC works with one version of the game: the original North American PlayStation 2 release.

| Game | Region | Serial | Version |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ratchet & Clank (2002, PlayStation 2) | NTSC-U | SCUS-97199 | 1.00 |

You'll find the serial printed on the disc itself.

## Not supported yet

- **PAL** (SCES-50916) and **Japanese** (SCPS-15037) discs, and **demo discs**.
- **Greatest Hits** discs, unless they match version 1.00. They share the same serial, so the launcher checks the
  game files to tell them apart.
- **Later releases:** the PS3 HD collection, the PS4 and PS Now versions, and the 2016 game.
- **The sequels:** Ratchet & Clank 2, 3 and Deadlocked.

If your disc isn't supported, the launcher tells you when you add it. [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/) explains
each message.

:::note[A version the launcher doesn't know?]
If the launcher says your Ratchet & Clank disc is a build it doesn't recognise, please report it and attach the log.
The line starting with `new build DB row:` is the part that helps.
:::
